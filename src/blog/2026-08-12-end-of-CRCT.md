# 🗓️ 2026-08-12 — 🏁 End of CRCT

The end of the Research Leave for Thematic Conversion (CRCT in french), which began almost
a year ago and whose purpose was to produce a stabilised version of the **GALACTIC**
framework, is fast approaching. Most of the work has now been completed, and the first
few weeks of the 2026-2027 academic semester will be devoted to finishing the plugins that
have not yet been updated. The next step will then be to recreate some existing
applications before imagining and developing new ones.

I would like to thank CNU 27 and my colleagues at the University of La Rochelle for
granting me this leave, which has enabled me to successfully carry out this work.
