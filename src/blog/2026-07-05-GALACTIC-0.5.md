# 🚀 GALACTIC 0.5 is here!

After more than three years of research, design, and development, we are proud to release
[**GALACTIC v0.5**](https://www.thegalactic.org/)—our most significant milestone to date.

This major release introduces a substantial evolution of the framework, with a particular
focus on helping users better understand and navigate the different layers of algebraic
structures implemented throughout GALACTIC. The architecture has been refined to make the
relationships between these algebraic abstractions clearer, more consistent, and easier
to explore.

Whether you are already using **GALACTIC** or discovering it for the first time,
version 0.5  provides a stronger foundation for building, extending, and experimenting
with algebraic  models.

We hope this release will make the underlying mathematical concepts more accessible while
offering a more robust and coherent development experience.

Explore **GALACTIC 0.5** and let us know what you think!
