# 🗓️ 2023-05-22 — Jeremy's thesis defence

Jérémy Richard defended his thesis on May 22, 2023. Here is a short summary.

This thesis studies tourist behaviour using a generic, interactive analysis
approach. It examines tourist trajectories in cities and museums. Experiments
collected movement data in a tourist city using GPS signals. The study first
focuses on reconstructing museum visitors' trajectories using indoor
positioning equipment, in a constrained environment.

A generic, multi-aspect semantic enrichment model then supplements individual
trajectories with contextual data such as the names of city districts and
museum rooms, outdoor weather and indoor mobile application data. The enriched
trajectories, called semantic trajectories, are analysed using formal concept
analysis and the GALACTIC platform. The platform analyses complex,
heterogeneous data as a hierarchy of groups of individuals sharing common
behaviours. Finally, the thesis examines the ReducedContextCompletion algorithm,
which enables interactive navigation of a concept lattice and lets analysts
focus on the aspects of the data they wish to explore.
